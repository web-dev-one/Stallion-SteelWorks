WHY CHOOSE OUR PIPE CORRALS?

Superior Materials

We source only the highest quality schedule 40 steel pipe from trusted American suppliers. This heavy-duty material provides a strength-to-weight ratio that far exceeds standard corral panels or wood construction.

Expert Craftsmanship

Each pipe corral is meticulously fabricated by our skilled welders who take pride in every weld and connection. Our precision cutting and fitting ensures perfect alignment and a professional finish.

Safety First Design

All our corrals feature smooth, rounded edges and secure fastening systems to eliminate injury risks to your horses. The rigid construction prevents bending or deformation that can create dangerous gaps.

Customization Options

We offer numerous customization options including various pipe diameters, multiple height options, gate configurations, feeders and waterers integration, custom finishes, and roof options for shaded areas.

Durability in Extreme Conditions

Our pipe corrals are designed to withstand the harsh Southwestern climate, from intense UV exposure to monsoon rains. The heavy-duty construction stands up to high winds and won't rust through like thinner materials.
