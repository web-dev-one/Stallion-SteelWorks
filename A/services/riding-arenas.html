How Your Arena Build Works

We keep the process simple and predictable, even on big, complex arenas. You’ll always know what’s happening, what’s next, and what we need from you.

Property Walk & Ride Plan

We walk the site, talk about wind, sun, drainage, and how you ride today—and how you want to ride in 5 years.

Concept, Size & Orientation

We recommend ideal arena dimensions, roof height, and orientation based on discipline, headcount, and budget.

Engineering & Fabrication

We handle engineering and fabricate your arena in steel tuned for your local code, soil, and weather loads.

Footing & Finishes

We coordinate with your footing contractor or help connect you with one so the surface matches the steel.

Installation & Handover

Our crews install with minimal disruption so you’re back in the saddle fast—and we stand behind it when the first storm hits.
